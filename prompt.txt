Entrants make one image meme about an AI agent that has gone rogue: a single picture, with any words written into it, whose joke lands on its own. Entries go in as image files on the bounty page. Once entries close, a judge model compares the pictures two at a time against the judge brief published with the bounty, weighing first whether the picture really is a funny meme about a rogue AI agent, then how fast the laugh lands, how specific and surprising the rogue turn is, how well made the picture is, and how fresh it feels. The top 10 entries share the $5 pot, and entry is free.
